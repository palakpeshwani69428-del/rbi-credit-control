Open Market Operations (OMO)
📖 Definition
Open Market Operations refer to the buying and selling of government securities (bonds, treasury bills) by the RBI in the open market to regulate liquidity and money supply in the banking system on a durable basis.
Two Types of OMO
🟢 OMO Purchase (Buying)
RBI buys government securities from banks → Injects liquidity → Money supply increases → Interest rates fall → Credit expands → Stimulates economic growth
🔴 OMO Sale (Selling)
RBI sells government securities to banks → Absorbs liquidity → Money supply decreases → Interest rates rise → Credit contracts → Controls inflation
Key Features
🎯 Purpose
Manage durable liquidity in the banking system and influence short-term interest rates to achieve monetary policy objectives
⚡ Mechanism
Liquidity and interest rates are inversely proportional — more liquidity lowers rates; less liquidity raises rates
📊 Instruments Used
Government of India dated securities, Treasury Bills, State Development Loans
