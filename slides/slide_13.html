Qualitative Method — 03
Moral Suasion
📖 Definition
Moral Suasion is a psychological and informal method by which the RBI persuades, advises, and requests commercial banks to follow its credit policies through oral or written communications — without using legal force or penalties.
📋 Forms of Moral Suasion
📝 Written Circulars
🗣️ Oral Advice
📢 Public Statements
⚠️ Warnings
📊 Guidelines
🤝 Appeals
RBI uses its moral authority and prestige as the central bank to influence bank behavior
No legal obligation — banks comply voluntarily out of respect and cooperative spirit
Frequently used during credit booms to discourage excessive lending in specific sectors
Effective when RBI has strong credibility and good relations with commercial banks
"The RBI's moral suasion works best when commercial banks trust the central bank's judgment and share its commitment to monetary stability and economic growth."
— Principle of Central Banking
✅ Advantages
• Flexible and quick to implement
• No legal complications
• Maintains good RBI-bank relations
• Cost-effective tool
❌ Limitations
• No legal enforcement power
• Banks may ignore advice
• Effectiveness varies by situation
• Depends on RBI's credibility
