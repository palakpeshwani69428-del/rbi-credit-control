Qualitative
Methods
Qualitative (Selective) methods regulate the direction and purpose of credit — steering it toward or away from specific sectors, without affecting total credit volume.
🎯 Primary Objective
To control the flow of credit into specific sectors — encouraging lending to priority areas (agriculture, SMEs) while discouraging speculative or unproductive lending.
⚡ Key Characteristic
These tools are discriminatory — they target specific sectors, borrowers, or purposes rather than the economy as a whole.
💡 Quantitative vs Qualitative: While quantitative tools control the amount of credit, qualitative tools control the purpose and direction — together they form a comprehensive credit management framework.
