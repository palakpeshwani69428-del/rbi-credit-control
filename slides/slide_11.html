Margin Requirements
📖 Definition
Margin Requirement is the difference between the market value of the security offered as collateral and the amount of loan granted against it. RBI prescribes minimum margin requirements for loans against specific securities.
Formula
Margin = Market Value of Security − Loan Amount

Loan Amount = Market Value × (1 − Margin %)
Higher margin → Borrower must provide more collateral → Less credit available → Discourages borrowing
Primarily used to control speculative lending — especially in stock markets and commodity trading
Different margins can be set for different sectors — selective in nature
Protects banks from losses if collateral value falls (risk management)

📈 Margin ↑
Less loan per unit of security → Credit contracts → Speculation curbed → Inflation controlled
📉 Margin ↓
More loan per unit of security → Credit expands → Investment encouraged → Growth stimulated
