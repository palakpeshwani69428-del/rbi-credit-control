Quantitative
Methods
Quantitative (General) methods regulate the total volume of credit in the economy without distinguishing between different uses or sectors of credit.
🎯 Primary Objective
To control the overall money supply and credit availability — expanding it during recession and contracting it during inflation.
⚡ Key Characteristic
These tools affect ALL sectors of the economy simultaneously and uniformly — they are non-discriminatory in nature.
