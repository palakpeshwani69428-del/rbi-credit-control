Bank Rate Policy
📌 Also known as: Discount Rate
📖 Definition
The Bank Rate is the minimum rate at which the Reserve Bank of India is prepared to re-discount approved bills of exchange or lend money to commercial banks against approved securities on a long-term basis without any collateral.
How It Works
When commercial banks need funds, they approach RBI and pay the Bank Rate as interest
Changes in Bank Rate cascade to market interest rates — affecting borrowing costs economy-wide
It is aligned with the Marginal Standing Facility (MSF) rate, forming the upper bound of RBI's policy corridor
Unlike Repo Rate, Bank Rate involves no repurchase agreement — it is a direct lending rate
Current Bank Rate (2026)
5.50%
As per RBI Monetary Policy Committee
📈 Bank Rate Increases →
Borrowing becomes expensive → Credit contracts → Inflation is controlled → Money supply decreases
📉 Bank Rate Decreases →
Borrowing becomes cheaper → Credit expands → Economic activity stimulated → Money supply increases
