Qualitative Method — 04
Direct Action
📖 Definition
Direct Action is the most coercive and powerful qualitative credit control tool, used by the RBI when commercial banks fail to comply with its guidelines, directives, or policies. It involves direct punitive measures against non-compliant banks.
⚡ Forms of Direct Action
🚫
Refusal to Rediscount: RBI refuses to rediscount bills of exchange or provide credit facilities to non-compliant banks
💸
Penal Interest Rate: RBI charges a penalty rate above the Bank Rate for credit demanded beyond prescribed limits
📋
Restriction of Credit: RBI restricts or withdraws credit facilities from banks violating norms
🔒
Cancellation of License: In extreme cases, RBI can cancel a bank's license or order its closure

⚠️ Last Resort Tool
Direct Action is used as a last resort when all other methods fail. It is not used in isolation — it supplements other credit control measures and is invoked only when banks persistently violate RBI norms.
